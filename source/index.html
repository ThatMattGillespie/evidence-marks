  • The card's top edge (chip, then "Full text · Systematic review") replaces the depth line and the "Cited as 2 in this chat" line. A source has one number per chat, so the chip already says which citation it is.
  • The byline moves under the title, in the card's grey sans: the year, then every author. The kind of paper leaves it, since the legend names it.
  • The depth note spells out what the legend's two words mean for this source, in a quiet panel. It carries what the old depth line's gloss said.
  • The full entry uses the same head with the larger title; its depth badge goes, and the sections and pages count stays.
  • Actions, the source address, Details, Keywords, Related organisations, the Abstract, the contents and the sections are unchanged.

Proposed, for the other kinds

The depth note matters most here. An abstract-only paper's actions still say "Read the abstract", and a web source has no full entry or record, so its note is the warning the old view gave in a paragraph.
