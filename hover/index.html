Six hover states for the citation chip

Today the chip is lifted 2px above the text so a line can draw in underneath it on hover. All six alternatives sit the chip on the line of text and need no room below it. Hover the three chips in each paragraph: an abstract, a full text and a web source.

Today

A line draws in under the chip, with a faint shadow. The chip rides 2px high to make room for it.

1 · Tint deepens

The chip's wash gets a little stronger. The quietest change, and the closest to how links behave elsewhere in the console.

2 · Edge sharpens

The border goes from a soft to a full accent line. Nothing moves or fills, so the reading stays still.

3 · Fills with the accent

The chip fills solid, a step before the dark "open" state. The clearest sign that it's clickable, and the loudest.

4 · Lights the claim

The sentence the chip supports gets a soft highlight, so you see exactly which words this source backs before you open it.

5 · Peek at the source

A small card shows the kind, title and year above the chip, so you can judge the source without opening the sidebar.

6 · The icon answers

The page inside the chip fills softly and its lines draw back, as if the page were turning. The edge sharpens with it.
