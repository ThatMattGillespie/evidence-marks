Before or after the full stop?

The same answer both ways, with the chip as built. Each chip stays on the line of the word before it in both. Point at a chip to see its box.

Before the punctuation · today

The agent writes it this way: the chip sits against the last word, then the full stop or comma. The style of numbered references in science and engineering (IEEE, Vancouver).

After the punctuation

The sentence closes, then the chip: the style of footnote numbers (Chicago) and of Wikipedia. The words read through to the full stop without a break.
