Authors after the year, without the language and abstract notes

The card's top edge already says "Abstract only", so the grey line under the title drops the language and the "Conclusions from abstract only" note, and the title loses its asterisk. Authors take their place after the year. The library stores each author as a full name, and some authors are organisations, so the three versions differ only in how a long list is cut short.

Things to notice

  • Card 3 has seven authors: the full list wraps to two or three lines, which is where the short forms earn their place.
  • Card 4 is written by an organisation, so it reads the same in every version.
  • What Works cards have no authors in the library, only a year, so they stay as they are.
  • The web card keeps its address. Its "Open web source, not reviewed" note is dropped here too, since the dashed edge and "External website" carry it; it could stay if the team wants the warning spelled out.
  • A paper with no year still says n.d. so the author never looks like it's in the year's place.
